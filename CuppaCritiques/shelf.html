My Reading Shelf

Sometimes I want a book I can disappear into for hours. Other times, a few pages of poetry with my iced coffee will do. Here’s a little of what you’ll find on my shelf.

Pick a book for your mood

  • Dungeon Crawler Carl by Matt Dinniman for a chaotic adventure with a scene-stealing cat. Read the spoiler-free review.
  • A Court of Thorns and Roses by Sarah J. Maas for faerie courts, dangerous bargains, and romance.
  • The Dark Between Stars by Atticus for short poems you can dip into whenever you have a few minutes.
