Dungeon Crawler Carl

By Matt Dinniman

When Earth becomes the set of an alien game show, Carl and Princess Donut, a cat with an attitude and a flair for the dramatic, have to survive a dungeon built for an audience. Their banter gives the book some breathing room between the traps, fights, and absurd game rules.

Pick this up if you like

  • Dark humor and chaotic adventures
  • Video game mechanics woven into the story
  • A cat who expects top billing
