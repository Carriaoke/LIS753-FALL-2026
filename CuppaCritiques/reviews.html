Dungeon Crawler Carl

By Matt Dinniman · Spoiler-free audiobook review

The easiest way to sell me on Dungeon Crawler Carl is to tell me there’s a cat named Princess Donut. The harder part is explaining how a story about an alien game show got me genuinely invested in whether a guy and his cat would make it through the next room.

Carl and Donut are the reason the whole thing works. Carl is trying to keep them alive, and Donut has opinions about how he should be doing it. Their banter gives the dungeon some personality, but it’s the way they start to look out for each other that gives the story its heart. The audiobook makes that dynamic even more fun. You can hear Donut’s outrage before she’s finished a sentence.

There’s a learning curve. The game rules, notifications, and stats come fast, and I found the story most engaging when Carl had a problem to solve and Donut had something to say about his solution. If you enjoy video game mechanics, there’s a lot to dig into. If you’re here for the cat, I get it.

Pick it up if you like

  • Dark humor and chaotic adventures
  • Ridiculous takes on reality TV
  • Survival stories with unexpectedly sweet friendships

I’d start with the audiobook.
